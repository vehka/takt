## Leads for next time

1. **Timber engine idle cost (46% DSP on Pi 3).** Biggest lever for xruns.
   Check what runs while nothing plays: delay, reverb, compressor, sidechain
   send buses, LFOs, and whether idle voices keep running. Measure with the
   idle procedure, removing one effect at a time in `Engine_Timber_Takt.sc`.
2. **Per-voice DSP cost.** Once idle is down, measure how DSP load grows with
   voices (1, 2, 4, 7 sample tracks at 16ths) to find where xruns start. A
   "realistic" stress level (4 sample + 2 MIDI chord tracks) would be useful.
3. **seqrun spikes on the shield** (p99 40 ms under normal load in new).
   Probe with `probe_params.lua` / `probe_tracks.lua` on the shield: `set_locks`
   → `params:set` → engine commands is the likely cost for sample tracks.
4. **Clock drift fix** (small upstream PR).
